PunchNow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your settings and clock records stay on your phone.
  • Only two things use the internet: the link ID download and the Google Play update check.

PunchNow ("the app") is an Android app that reminds you to clock in and out and opens your attendance app for you. It is made by OwlCraftLab ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: I don't collect your personal data.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to me or to anyone else:

  • Your settings: schedule, reminder times, days off, language and the action that opens your attendance app.
  • A record of when you tapped Clock in or Clock out, and whether it worked.
  • If you use a link ID, the ID itself, so it can be shown in Settings.

This data stays on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The app does not back it up.

Information that leaves your phone

  • Link ID: when you enter a link ID and passcode, the app downloads an encrypted settings file named after your ID from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). I don't run a server and I don't see or keep anything about these downloads. GitHub, which hosts the file, may keep its own logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Your passcode is used on your phone to unlock the file and is never sent.
  • App updates: the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

I don't sell or share your information, and I don't use advertising or analytics services.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen reminders: to ring a reminder at the time you choose, even when your phone is locked.
  • Run after restart: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet: for the link ID download and update check described above.
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts.

The app looks at which apps are installed only on your phone, so you can pick the one to open. That list is never sent anywhere. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Your attendance app

PunchNow only opens your attendance app. It cannot see what you do inside it, and it does not send anything to it or receive anything from it, unless you set up an action that does so yourself.

Children

The app is for workplace use and is not aimed at children. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app, and everything it saved is deleted. Because I don't hold your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
